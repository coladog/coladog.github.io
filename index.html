I am a CS PhD student @ Stony Brook U, starting 2023 fall. I am working on practical formal verification for performant distributed system, advised by Prof. Shuai Mu and Prof. Omar Chowdhurry . I also work closely with Zihao Zhang and Christa Jenkins.
Before joining Stony Brook, I earned my M.Sc. (2023) and Honours B.Sc. (2021) degrees in Computer Science from StFX University, Canada. I was advised by Prof. Man Lin. My research focused on CPU power management within the Linux kernel on embedded devices.
