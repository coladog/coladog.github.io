I'm a CS PhD candidate at Stony Brook University (since Fall 2023), advised by Prof. Shuai Mu. My research is about making formal reasoning practical, in two directions: designing novel formal abstractions that bring real, high-performance systems within reach of verification; and using formal methods and symbolic reasoning to build AI agents that are both more capable and more efficient.

Before joining Stony Brook, I completed my M.Sc. (2023) and Honours B.Sc. (2021) in Computer Science at StFX University in Canada, where I was advised by Prof. Man Lin. I worked on designing and implementing reinforcement learning agents that run inside the Linux kernel for energy-efficient CPU frequency management.

I enjoy many forms of art, and I see computer systems as one of them. I do some painting.
